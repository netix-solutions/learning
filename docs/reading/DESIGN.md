# SunSharp reading library

Audience: PK–5 children using iPad, phone, or desktop. The primary job is choosing an inviting book, reading comfortably, and thinking about its meaning.

Design plan: real illustrated book covers, an open-book reading spread, warm paper (#fffdf5), deep ink (#203552), navy controls (#204969), and sunlight amber (#f3bd55). Retain SunSharp's friendly Nunito headings; older-reader prose uses Georgia. Make artwork the memorable element; avoid a dashboard of metric cards. Library shelves use understated spine and paper-edge details. Reader controls remain separate from prose and support 44px or larger targets, keyboard, safe areas, large text and reduced motion.

Plan review: generic feature cards and a stats-heavy hero would compete with stories. Replace them with one illustrated invitation and a real bookshelf. Avoid an elaborate 3D page flip; a short opacity transition keeps reading stable. Phone layouts place a compact illustration above text and retain visible navigation. Readability takes precedence over forcing large text into a fixed screen.

Content: eight original fiction titles for each of PK, K, and grades 1–5, eight recurring worlds, 56 unique story illustrations plus eight collection illustrations. Editorial grade recommendations are not formal Lexile or decodability certification. PK/K are suitable for shared reading. Deeper evidence, inference, perspective and source questions appear in older grades.

Comprehension: one question after page two where the text supports it, remaining questions after the ending. First answers and explicit look-back support are recorded; repeat practice does not inflate the first score. Retelling is a grown-up discussion, not automatically scored. Time is active engagement, not a measure of reading accuracy or mastery.

Foundational references: IES Practice Guide 21 (daily connected text); Reading Rockets story maps and strategies to promote comprehension. https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/practiceguide/21 ; https://www.readingrockets.org/classroom/classroom-strategies/story-maps
